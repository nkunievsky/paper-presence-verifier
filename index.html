What does verification establish?

The signature ties the PDF to the duration attested by its registry. The camera requirement checks that each device contributing time has a verified three-frame window for every complete UTC minute in its disclosed recording runs, with at least one window. Partial minutes and the time between samples rely on the recorder. Estimated engagement is a model output.

To inspect a signed allocation that lacks camera samples, clear the camera requirement. The verifier still checks all attached proofs.

For links, use the downloadable PDF or certificate. Some repositories serve a cover page, rebuild PDFs, or block browser downloads. You can always select a downloaded file.
